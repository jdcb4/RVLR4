## Short description

## Full description

Bring everyone together with RVLRY: a collection of games to play around one phone.

WHO WHAT WHERE
Describe people, things and places while your team races against the clock. Choose your teams, rounds and difficulty, then pass the phone to the next player.

HAT GAME
Build a shared collection of clues and play through rounds with changing ways to describe them.

IMPOSTER
Find the player who doesn't share the secret. Reveal roles privately, then discuss and make your choice together.

TRIVIA
Enjoy a cooperative quiz, like doing the newspaper quiz together. Choose 10, 20 or 30 questions, select categories and difficulty, and reveal answers after each question or at the end. Write optional shared answers in end-reveal mode, mark answers together, and see your score and recap.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose from five visual themes. Easy, Medium and Hard options let you adjust supported games to your group. Turn on Simple mode in app settings for easy words and questions in new games and automatic suggestions.

PLAY OFFLINE
No account or internet connection is needed to play. Game progress is saved on your phone so you can return after an interruption. RVLRY has no ads or in-app purchases.

Includes 600 bundled trivia questions across general knowledge, history, geography, science and nature, arts and literature, film and TV, music, and sport and games.

CHECK FOR MORE CONTENT
An optional check in Settings downloads available word and trivia updates. Playing the bundled games works offline.
