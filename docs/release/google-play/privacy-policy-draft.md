## Information on your device

RVLRY stores game setup, player and team names you enter, custom clues, written quiz answers, scores, progress, selected questions and app preferences on your device. This information allows games to resume after an interruption. You can use nicknames; an account, real name or email address is not required to play.

The app does not send this gameplay information to the publisher or other players' devices. RVLRY does not operate a cloud account or cloud-save service. Android backup is disabled for this app.

## Collection, sharing and advertising

The app does not transmit gameplay data, sell personal data, display advertising, or include analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or location. Fonts, artwork and initial word/Trivia catalogues are bundled with the app.

If you tap Check for new words, the app requests public word and Trivia update files from content.jboxgames.com, hosted by Cloudflare. The app sends no player names, answers, saved games, account identifiers or cookies with these requests. As with other HTTPS services, Cloudflare receives network information such as your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the service. Application request logging/Worker observability is disabled; provider-level processing and retention must be confirmed before publishing the final policy and Play declaration. Updates are manual, and playing does not require an internet connection.

## Children and shared play

Kids mode selects child-suitable words and questions for shared play. It does not ask for or verify a child's date of birth, create child profiles, or transmit information about children to the publisher. People sharing a phone may see names, scores and saved game information on that phone. Screenshots are permitted; any sharing through the phone's operating system is controlled by the person using the device.

## Removing information

To remove all locally stored games and preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > RVLRY > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall RVLRY. This resets the app and removes its local data. RVLRY has no server-side user account to delete.

## Contact and external services

If you choose to email the publisher for help, the information you send is processed by the publisher and email service for responding to that request. Contact practices and retention must be finalized by the publisher before publishing this draft. Google Play and Android operate under their own policies for store and operating-system services.
