Recommended: a compact two-level tracker

The round segments show the whole game. The labeled team strip shows this round: complete, current, next. Text and borders carry the meaning as well as color. The tracker is informational; tapping it must never jump the game forward.

During play it stays quiet. At handoff and recap it expands into plain-language instructions. Setup uses Settings → Teams → Review instead, because rounds have not started.

Alternative: show the whole game as a grid

This makes every turn visible, but takes more space from words and controls. I would put it behind “Game progress,” rather than keep it on every screen.
