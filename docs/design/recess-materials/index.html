Recess, off the grid.

Three ways to give RVLRY a little more paper, personality and play.

Interactive material studies
A

Sticker stack

Just enough mischief.

A measured first step: slight rotation, sticker rims and short shadows. Familiar rectangles keep the game calm; most character lives on the picker and handoff.

B

Cut-paper playground

The sweet spot.

Recommended

Asymmetric corners, overlapping sheets and a bigger shadow step. Enough shape to feel made by hand, with straight text and steady controls during a timed turn.

C

Craft-table collage

Turn the personality up.

Scissor-cut edges, taped paper and a looser composition. The most distinctive option; extra edges add visual activity, especially around lists and small controls.
