## Final accepted set and replacements

The saved checklist above is the pre-draft candidate plan, not the shipped answer list. Source support and coordinator review changed several candidates: Amélie/Paris became Jurassic Park/Steven Spielberg; Toto became Dorothy's Kansas home; Shaun's question asks for Shaun rather than leaking Sheep in the answer; montage became The Artist; four director slots became Rear Window's photographer, Roma's Mexico City setting, the Spanish Civil War setting of Pan's Labyrinth, and Michelle Yeoh's Everything Everywhere All at Once Oscar. Green Destiny was rejected as overly small film detail and is absent from the accepted set.

Music replaces the planned ukulele/lullaby/semitone slots with double bass/pitch/a cappella. Non-classical hard additions include Carlos Santana, Ladysmith Black Mambazo, Björk, Fela Kuti, Édith Piaf and A. R. Rahman; Pavarotti also replaces one composer slot. Sport replaces the helmet candidate with pedals, chess with Ernő Rubik, and Othello with Scrabble's seven-tile bonus. The Rubik clue omits the puzzle's eponymous name. These changes preserve every quota and expand subject variety.
