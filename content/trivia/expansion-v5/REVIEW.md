## Editorial review

The coordinator read every new question, canonical answer and alias list. Category workers checked every accepted answer against opened sources and saved evidence in the adjacent review files. Checks are AI-assisted, not independent human editorial approval. Ages 8–12 familiarity and difficulty remain editorial estimates. No historical candidate bank was imported.

Review changes included reducing film-director templates, replacing a niche sword-name fact with a broader film award fact, removing a surname leak from the cube-inventor clue, narrowing book-title aliases, and replacing unsupported candidates rather than counting failed source opens. Diacritics, clean text, question marks and alias identity are schema-checked. Existing manual marking handles spelling/punctuation variants not eligible for exact auto-marking.
